# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 10 November 2026

- Uses "新手秒懂期权 · Options Made Simple for Beginners" as the bilingual name.
- Addressed the reader as "your options bill" throughout the page.

- Packaged as a static web project ready to upload to GitHub.
- Made the numbers on the strategy cards compact labels, and enlarged the explanation area and text below them.
- Adjusted the space balance between the chart, the slider, and the settlement area.
- Matched explanation amounts to the parameter colors; cost information uses purple.
- Enlarged the axis, legend, and markers, and adjusted marker positions to reduce overlap.
- Kept the original page copy, strategy math, mode switching, price slider, and other interactions.
- Added usage instructions, a calculation-scope note, and a GitHub Pages publishing guide.

Pre-expiry option market prices, time value, implied volatility, independent share and contract counts, and broker margin and liquidation simulation are not yet implemented.
